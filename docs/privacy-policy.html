QRify Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

QRify ("the app") is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator published by IntelliCraft. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

QRify does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device in real time and are never saved or uploaded.

Photos and gallery

If you choose to scan a code from an image, you pick the image with the system photo picker. The image is read on your device only to find a code and is not uploaded. If you tap "Save", the generated QR code or barcode is saved to your device's gallery in a "QRify" album.

Scan and generation history

Your history of scanned and generated codes is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with the "Clear" button on the History screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Actions you choose

When you tap an action on a scan result, such as opening a link, calling a number, sending an email or SMS, adding a contact, or sharing a code, the app hands that information to the relevant app on your phone (for example, your browser, phone, or contacts app). Those apps are covered by their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

QRify does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. Code scanning uses on-device barcode recognition and does not send your images anywhere.

Children

QRify is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
